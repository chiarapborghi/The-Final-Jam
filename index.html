The School production

The school production has been a long-standing school activity cherished by the students. Each year, a unique musical production is chosen and recreated by the pupils and staff of The British School. The school production requires countless hours of rehearsals, all of which occur after school 2-3 times a week, from the start of the year up to the performance date. A tremendous amount of effort is put into the planning of scenes, costuming, and props; every detail is carefully considered and mapped out over the course of the rehearsals. Our ultimate goal is to produce an unforgettable performance and entertain all members of the audience.

The Final Jam

This year's chosen musical is “The Final Jam”, an adaptation inspired by the hit 2008 Disney film “Camp Rock” using an original script, written by the school production team. The story follows the life of a shy, teenage girl named Mitchie Torres, who desperately dreams of attending a prestigious music summer camp and pursuing her singing career. When she unexpectedly gets the opportunity to attend this summer, she meets multiple new people, including spoiled pop phenomenon Shane Grey, and struggles to navigate this new world filled with rich and famous campers. Shane overhears Mitchie singing in secret, but does not manage to see her face. Star-struck, he sets out to find the mystery girl whose song he can't forget.
